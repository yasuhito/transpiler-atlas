Interpretation

  • Quality combines 2Q count and 2Q depth. Scores use equal family weights and the same run's Qiskit baseline of 100.
  • Speed uses a 1 ms floor. A score is shown only when every required trial passes validation.
  • pytket does not accept the seed in this pipeline. Its three seed slots are repeated measurements, not independent seeded configurations.
  • Timing includes pipeline construction and compilation. Parsing, export, conversion for validation, and QCEC are excluded.
  • No enforced memory cap, exclusive host, or CPU frequency control. A first-use cost can enter the first of three timing repetitions.
